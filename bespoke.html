A shoe for one foot. Yours.

Most shoes are ready to wear — a size, an average, a guess. Ours are not. Everything here is made after it is ordered, and only what you order. Some of it is made to your measurements. All of it is made for you.

The made-to-measure line of Swing Love — for anyone who loves a well-made shoe, dancer or not.

Made to order

Choose from our models, leathers and soles. Your pair is cut and made after you order it — nothing is taken from a shelf.

Made to measure

We work from your measurements, and where a foot needs it, your own last. For sizing that is awkward, wide, narrow, or simply particular.

Fully bespoke

For shoe lovers

Not only for dancing.

We began with dancers, and we have not forgotten them. But a shoe made this carefully belongs anywhere. If you have ever turned a shoe over to look at how the sole is stitched, this is for you.

Derbies, loafers, brogues, boots. Goodyear welted for a lifetime of wear. McKay stitched for a lighter line. Patina finished by hand in the colour you choose — or one we arrive at together.

The process

1 · A conversation
Tell us what you are after, and how you will wear it. We will tell you what is possible.
2 · Measurements
A simple guide we send you, or a trace of the foot. We will help you through it — this is the part most people worry about, and it is easier than it looks.
3 · Materials
Leathers, soles, lining, colour. Swatches are sent so you can feel the choice in your hands.
4 · The making
Twelve to fourteen weeks. You are welcome to ask for photographs along the way — we like sharing them.
5 · Delivery
Finished, polished, wrapped, and engraved with your name if you wish. Made to be worn for years, and repaired when it needs it.
