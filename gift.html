Gifts

Give a pair that has to be made.

A fitting to book, a model and a leather to choose together, a name in the sole. A gift that begins as a conversation — and ends as a pair made for one person.

A gift with a beginning

More than a box.

Most gifts are chosen in a hurry and forgotten by spring. This one starts a conversation, and ends with a pair made for one person on earth.

It suits a wedding, a milestone, a thank-you that has to mean something. We keep it simple to give — and a pleasure to receive.

How a gift works

1 · A card, and a fitting
We send a hand-written card that books their fitting whenever they are ready.
2 · Their choices
Model, leather, sole, colour, a name engraved in the sole. It becomes theirs the moment they begin.
3 · The making
Fitted, cut and made by hand. Twelve to fourteen weeks from the fitting.
4 · Delivered
Wrapped and sent wherever they are — so it arrives once as a card, and again as a pair.
