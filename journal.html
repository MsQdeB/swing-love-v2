On patina, and why no two pairs match.

Colour is not painted on at the tannery and left at that. We build it here, by hand, in layers — creams, waxes, a little pressure — until the leather has depth rather than flatness. Which is why your pair will never look exactly like the pair in the photograph.

The character

Ái, in three moods.

Mostly cheerful, occasionally thoughtful, and always a little bit in a hurry — usually with a basket of shoes on the front of her scooter. Ái is not the brand; Châu is. But she is the workshop’s small, painted spirit, and this is where she likes to hang out.

If you see her on a shoe bag, a sticker, or the corner of a page — that’s on purpose.
