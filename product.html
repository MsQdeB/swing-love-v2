Materials

Full-grain and top-grain leather only — never “genuine leather.” Brocade, where used, comes from a co-operative of Thái women in Nghệ An province. Lined in soft leather; a leather insole with arch support and a bed of padding for comfort.

Construction & sole

Choose McKay stitched for a slimmer, lighter shoe with more flex — easy on a dancer’s foot — or Goodyear welted for a sturdier, more water-resistant shoe. Either way the sole is stitched, not glued, and can be replaced again and again.

Fit & sizing

Made to order runs on our house lasts; made to measure is built to your numbers. Not sure of your size? See our illustrated measuring guide — we’ll help you through it.

Making time & care

Allow 12–14 weeks. Your shoes arrive polished and wrapped; we’re glad to send photographs along the way. Look after them and they’ll last years — see care & repair.
