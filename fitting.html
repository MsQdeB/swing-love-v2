Measuring at home

It takes about ten minutes, and it is easier than most people expect. You need a tape measure, a sheet of paper and a pencil — the guide does the rest.

1 · Length
Measure your foot from heel to longest toe, standing.
2 · Trace
Draw around each foot on paper, for the shape as well as the size.
3 · Width & more
A few more numbers, and photos if you can — they tell us what a tape measure can’t.
4 · Send
Message us the numbers. We check them, and only then begin.

Unsure at any step? Send a photo and we’ll walk you through it. We would rather spend an extra message than remake a pair.
