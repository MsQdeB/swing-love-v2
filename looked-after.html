Looked after

Made to be kept, not replaced.

A stitched leather shoe does not have to end when the sole does. We keep your numbers, refine the fit, and can bring a worn pair back to life.

How it works

We remember your feet.

You should not have to start from scratch every time. Once we have your measurements, we keep them — so the second pair is easier than the first, and the third easier again.

Your measurements
Kept on file after your first fitting, so nothing is guessed twice.
The next pair
We cut from your numbers and any notes from the last pair, so it fits a little better each time.
Between pairs
Tell us how they wear. We adjust the fit and the design for the next one.
When they wear
We recraft and resole every pair we make — see recraft & resole.
The idea

Buy fewer pairs. Wear them for years.

A stitched, hand-lasted shoe can be repaired, and a repaired shoe can be worn again. That is the point of making it this way — you are not buying a season, you are buying years.
