Recraft & resole

The sole wears out. The shoe doesn’t have to.

Because our soles are stitched — welted or McKay, never glued — they can be replaced. The upper, which only improves with age, carries on.

What we do

A cemented shoe is finished when the sole is. Ours is not.

The sole comes off, a new one goes on, and years of patina stay exactly where they were earned.

Resole
A new leather or rubber sole, stitched on, and the waist reshaped by hand.
Recraft
New heel, refreshed edges, seams re-stitched where they’ve worn.
Restore
Cleaned, fed and re-polished — the patina brought back, not covered.

How to send them

1 · A photo
Send a photo of the pair and what concerns you. We’ll tell you honestly what is worth repairing — and what isn’t.
2 · A quote
We come back with a price and a time. No surprises.
3 · The post
You send the shoes; we recraft them here, and send them back.

This is the reason the shoes cost what they cost: you are buying years, not a season.
