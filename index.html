I am Châu. I began Swing Love as a dancer who could never quite find a shoe that fit — not really fit. Years, and many pairs, later, we are still here.

Still making shoes by hand, in Việt Nam, the only way we know how. Along the way the craft has changed hands, the workshop has changed, and so have we — but the intention has not.

Quality over quantity. Always.

The workshop

Every pair is made here, in Việt Nam.

Not bought in and relabelled. Our shoes are cut, lasted and finished by hand in our own workshop — leather outsoles and stacked heels, stitched so they can be resoled for years.

We work in small batches, and we pay for the work properly. Ten pairs made well, not a thousand made fast.

Looked after

Made to be kept, not replaced.

We keep your measurements on file, so your next pair fits even better than this one. And when a sole wears through, we can recraft it — the shoe goes on, and so does the fit.

Fittings

Measure at home — or meet us in Đà Nẵng or Hội An.

We don’t have a shop. We do have time: measure at home with our guide and send us the numbers, or meet us in person if you’re nearby. Either way, we get the fit right before anything is cut.
