Questions

Answered plainly.

The things people ask us most, in plain words — materials, making, measuring, and shipping.

Do you use genuine leather?

In short: no. “Genuine leather” sounds like it means high quality — it does not. It is the lowest grade, made from leather scraps bound with plastic. We source only full-grain and top-grain leather, the best grades there are for footwear.

Why leather? Wouldn’t vegan leather be more sustainable?

It’s a fair question. Most vegan leathers — from pineapple to mango — are still plastic-based. For a small accessory that may be fine; for a breathable shoe that must last and flex, plastic leather is not durable and it holds odour. Leather takes work and chemicals to make, which is exactly why we buy ours from reliable sources and use it well: a leather shoe, properly made, lasts years and can be repaired.

How can I measure my own feet?

We send you a simple guide — and often a trace to stand on — and we walk you through it. It is the part most people worry about, and it is easier than it looks. If anything is unclear, write to us and we’ll help.

How long does a pair take?

Made-to-order and made-to-measure pairs take around twelve to fourteen weeks. We are making them one pair at a time, by hand — and we would rather be honest about the time than rush it.

You’re based in Việt Nam. Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide from Việt Nam. See our shipping policy for current rates and times, or write to us and we’ll work it out with you.

Can I have something made that isn’t in the collection?

Very often, yes. Almost everything can be adjusted, and some things can be drawn from scratch. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll tell you honestly what is possible.
